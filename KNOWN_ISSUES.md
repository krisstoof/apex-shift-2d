# Known Issues

## v0.1.2 Prototype Limitations

This build is still a prototype. The items below are expected limitations or known rough edges for testers.

### UI and presentation
- Some screens are intentionally utilitarian and may not have final art or polish.
- Debug overlays and performance readouts are meant for testing, not final player-facing use.

### Inventory and storage
- Inventory and storage box flows are functional but still subject to balance and UX changes.
- Item presentation may feel simple compared with a finished release.

### Save and load
- Save data currently focuses on the systems needed for the prototype.
- New or changed state may need follow-up validation after future feature work.

### Gameplay and balance
- Creature behavior, drops, and resource tuning are still under active iteration.
- Balance changes should be treated as expected prototype work unless they break core flows.

### What testers do not need to report as new bugs
- The prototype look of UI elements.
- The intentionally simple inventory and storage flows.
- Debug output that supports manual verification.
- Minor tuning issues that do not block normal play.
